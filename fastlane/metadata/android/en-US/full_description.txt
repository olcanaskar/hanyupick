HanyuPick is an offline-first flashcard app for the official HSK 1-6 Chinese vocabulary lists. The whole 5,000-word library is bundled in the app: 150 + 150 + 300 + 600 + 1,300 + 2,500 words, with ids 1-5000 and no gaps.

PRACTICE IN SIX DIRECTIONS
- Hanzi to Pinyin
- Hanzi to English
- Pinyin to Hanzi
- Pinyin to English
- English to Hanzi
- English to Pinyin
Every question is drawn from the same HSK level, so the wrong options are never from an easier level. After you answer, the card shows the third field as a hint and an example sentence that contains the word.

A MISTAKE POOL, NOT A RESET BUTTON
Words you get wrong go into a review pool for that direction. Answer correctly and the word leaves the pool; the count on the home screen shows what is left.

THE WHOLE LIBRARY, NO ACCOUNT
No sign-up, no server, no ads, no tracking. Progress stays on your phone. The app works in aeroplane mode, except for search by drawing, which sends the strokes to Google's public Input Tools service when you use it.

MORE
- Vocabulary search by hanzi, pinyin or meaning
- Handwriting pad to look up a word by drawing it
- Custom decks imported from a JSON file
- Mastery, streaks and an activity calendar
- Speak a word aloud with the device's text-to-speech engine
- Haptics and speech volume settings
- A light, readable design in the app's red palette

HanyuPick is free software, released under the MIT license. The source code is at https://github.com/olcanaskar/hanyupick.
